# Founder’s Note: The Next Chapter for AgentFlight & Our OpenAny Partnership

**By: Akandwanaho Ivan Alvin**  
*Creator & Independent Owner, AgentFlight*  
*November 11, 2026*

---

When I created **AgentFlight**, my goal was simple: bring the rigorous reliability of aerospace flight recorders to autonomous AI agents.

Building autonomous systems is exhilarating, but debugging them in production is often terrifying. Agents get stuck in subtle loops, repeat identical mistakes, swallow errors, and quietly burn through hundreds of dollars in API credits while you sleep.

Today, I am proud to share a major milestone: **AgentFlight has formed an official commercial partnership with OpenAny**, founded by Pistis.

### Why This Partnership Matters

OpenAny built something extraordinary—a single gateway giving developers access to 128+ AI models and over 3,200 external tools.

But expansive capabilities require uncompromising safety. That is where AgentFlight fits:
- We protect developers from burning prepaid balances with pre-execution circuit breakers.
- We halt runaway agent loops before they turn into billing nightmares.
- We give developers sub-microsecond offline replay so they can test complex agent behaviors in CI/CD without paying a single cent in recurring API fees.

### Our First Commercial Revenue Stream

As an independent creator from Uganda building open-source AI developer tools for the global community, sustainability is vital.

Under this partnership:
- AgentFlight receives a **permanent 30% revenue share** on usage attributed to AgentFlight via our verified header: `X-OpenAny-Partner-Id: agentflight`.
- OpenAny provides a dedicated partner dashboard for real-time visibility.
- AgentFlight remains **100% independently owned, free, open-source, and MIT-licensed**.

You will never be locked into OpenAny. AgentFlight works seamlessly with local models, open-source endpoints, and any provider of your choice. But if you choose to build with OpenAny, AgentFlight ensures your spend is controlled, your trajectories are recorded, and your development supports the continuous evolution of this project.

Thank you to Pistis and the OpenAny team for recognizing the value of developer-first safety tooling. And thank you to every engineer testing and running AgentFlight across the globe.

Let's build reliable agents.
